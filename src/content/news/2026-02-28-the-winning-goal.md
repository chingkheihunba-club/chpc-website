The 32nd Governor’s Cup Invitation final, on 28 February 2026 at Mapal Kangjeibung, was a rematch. A fortnight
earlier, X-Polo Club, Wangkhei, had beaten the club in the Dr Ksh Chourjit Singh Inter-District final. This time
the club took an early lead, X-Polo came back in the third chukker, and it came down to the fourth.

The last goal of the final came from Leishemba Takhellambam, in white. It made the score 7–6 and won the club the
Governor’s Cup. He finished the final as the club’s top scorer, with three goals.
